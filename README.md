### CHEM-000224: Uniform maximum atomic excess charge
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Prove that a nucleus of charge \(Z\) can bind at most \(Z+C\) electrons, with \(C\) independent of \(Z\), for the full nonrelativistic many-electron Coulomb Hamiltonian.
- **Status:** The Hartree–Fock analogue is established, while the interacting Schrödinger problem remains open. A 2025 result gives the explicit bound \(N_c<1.1185Z+4Z^{1/3}\) for \(Z\ge4\), which still permits an excess increasing with nuclear charge.
